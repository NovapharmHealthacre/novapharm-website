Controlled onboarding

No application data is collected here.

NovaPharm account onboarding requires an approved managed application environment, due-diligence review and controlled document handling. Do not enter or upload company, licence, bank, credit, responsible-person or quality information on a static or unverified form.

Account status

Preparing documents, making an enquiry or discussing a commercial opportunity does not create an approved trading account, confirm product availability or permit regulated supply. Due diligence, credit review and applicable regulatory checks remain required.

When managed onboarding is activated, NovaPharm will provide the approved application route through a verified business channel.
