Current regulatory boundary

Regulated pharmaceutical wholesale supply has not commenced. Any future supply depends on required local authorisations, product-specific permissions, qualified suppliers, GDP/quality controls and permitted buyer channels. UK permissions do not confer distribution rights in other countries.
