THE WORKSPACES

From the molecule
to the market.

Specialised perspectives organised around a shared medicine identity, with each capability released only when its underlying data and controls are ready.

01

Medicine identity

Unify VTM, VMP, AMP, BNF, ATC, SNOMED CT and regulatory identifiers. Make ambiguous or unmatched records explicit.

02

Evidence & regulation

Trace licences, evidence, scientific literature, regulatory developments, source dates and jurisdiction-specific interpretation.

03

Market & geography

Examine observed demand, geographical variation, market structure and commercial context without confusing estimates with recorded facts.

04

Forecasts & signals

Build explainable forecasts with accepted history, assumptions, backtests, intervals and monitored changes. No unsupported AI score.

05

Supply, pricing & tenders

Connect sourcing intelligence, procurement notices, reimbursement, pricing and supply developments, with source-specific access rules.

06

Biotech & capital

Investigate assets, clinical programmes, company developments and capital signals as a distinct research layer.

07

Portfolio & decisions

Bring together opportunities, commercial assumptions, alternatives, watchlists and accountable decision records.

08

Reports & integrations

Make governed outputs reusable through reports and future partner integrations, with permissions and traceability preserved.

These are intended product workspaces, not a statement that each module or dataset is commercially operational today.

DESIGNED FOR TRUST

Every answer should show
what kind of answer it is.

Our evidence model separates what has been observed from what has been calculated, forecast, assumed or recommended.

01Observed fact

What a named, dated source actually reports.

02Derived metric

What a transparent and reproducible method calculates.

03Model forecast

What a specified model predicts, with uncertainty and validation.

04Commercial assumption

What a user or business scenario elects to assume.

05Recommendation

What a decision-support process proposes for human assessment.

Built around source provenance, freshness, jurisdiction, role-based access, explicit uncertainty and human review. An unresolved match stays unresolved.
