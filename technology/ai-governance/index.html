Responsible AI

AI may support review; it cannot become the authority.

Approved sources, capability boundaries, privacy, citations, abstention and accountable human decisions.

01

Approved corpus

Only registered public or authorised internal sources may support an answer.

02

Privacy

Credentials, patient data, portal records and confidential documents are excluded from public retrieval.

03

Citation

Material output must retain its source, passage, date and evidence status.

04

Abstention

The system must say when approved evidence is insufficient.

05

Human authority

AI cannot approve products, licences, suppliers, quality records or releases.

06

Audit

Internal use records model, policy, user, purpose and outcome without logging secrets.

Prohibited

No medical, safety or regulated decision is delegated to a public assistant.

Patient-specific advice, adverse-event handling, quality-defect decisions, licence conclusions, product release, pricing, inventory promises and confidential retrieval remain outside the public AI boundary.
