Provenance before prediction

Know what you're looking at.

The interface is functional. The sample numbers are not real observations. A production system must make that distinction unmistakable.

Geography boundaries

ONS regions, December 2025, super-generalised English regional boundaries. Official map geometry is requested from the ONS ArcGIS service. View source.

Prescribing figures

All nine regional rates, totals, indicators and trends are illustrative. These are not NHSBSA observations and must not be used in a real commercial or clinical decision.

NHS source connections

English Prescribing Data and Prescription Cost Analysis: not active in this preview. Secondary Care Medicines Data and population denominators: unvalidated here.

Methodology and forecasts

Example index = sample region rate divided by the highest example rate. The timeline is a synthetic presentation device. No forecasting, statistical inference or model validation is represented.

Data principles: observed fact, derived metric, model forecast, commercial assumption and recommendation must be separately identified and auditable.
