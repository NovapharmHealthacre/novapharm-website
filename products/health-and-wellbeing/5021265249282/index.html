Product information

Ingredients

Calcium carbonate, magnesium hydroxide, potato starch, zinc sulphate, tablet coating containing hydroxypropylmethylcellulose, isomalt, medium-chain triglycerides and calcium carbonate, vitamin D3, purified talc, sodium borate, crosslinked cellulose gum, manganese sulphate, copper sulphate and sodium selenite.

Directions

Two tablets a day with the main meal, swallowed with water or a cold drink.

Warnings, allergens and storage

Food supplement, not for children. Do not exceed the advised daily dose; consult a professional if medically supervised or allergic to an ingredient. Keep dry below 25°C and away from children. Manufacturer states that the production facility may handle nuts. Verify the actual UK pack. Refer to the actual packaging supplied for the complete allergen statement.
