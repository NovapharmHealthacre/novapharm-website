Scientific verification principles

Separate problem from execution

Record scientific question, source, Hamiltonian or circuit, solver and evidence as separate versioned elements.

Independent classical baselines

Compare numerical results against exact diagonalization or a suitable classical chemistry/optimization reference.

Measure uncertainty and noise

Separate finite-shot uncertainty, hardware or simulated noise, and model approximation error.

Repeatable and falsifiable experiments

Pin source, input hash, engine version, seed, circuit, geometry, basis and test tolerances.

Fail closed on data rights and safety

No patient data, licensed pharmaceutical datasets, third-party model egress or regulated decisions in unapproved research.

Human-gated promotion

A validated schema or passing CI cannot grant clinical, customer, source-rights or production authority.

A valid software schema does not establish scientific truth, clinical utility, hardware calibration or quantum advantage. Every research claim needs independently reproducible classical baselines and qualified human review.
