THE PRINCIPLE

Every detail
matters.

A medicine is more than its name. Its identity, evidence, source, handling and accountable decisions must remain connected through every hand-off.

Commercial interest opens a question. It does not establish a right to supply, the quality of the evidence or readiness for regulated activity.

OUR EVIDENCE MODEL

Six connected decisions.
One coherent view.

Complex pharmaceutical opportunities need more than a checklist. Each evidence axis should lead to a traceable, accountable next decision.

  1. 01

    Identity

    Know the exact medicine.

    Product identity, formulation, presentation, supply rights and the proposed regulated route must be unambiguous.

  2. 02

    Evidence

    Make every claim traceable.

    Dossier status, authorisations, approved information, quality records and stability evidence determine what can be considered.

  3. 03

    Source

    Qualify every hand-off.

    Manufacturers, rights holders, authorised suppliers and specialist providers retain clear responsibilities and oversight.

  4. 04

    Condition

    Protect the product requirements.

    Packaging, storage, transport, monitoring, custody and exception review follow product-specific evidence.

  5. 05

    Demand

    Plan without pretending to predict.

    Approved assumptions, lead times, constraints and uncertainty inform continuity planning without suggesting guaranteed availability.

  6. 06

    Decision

    Keep a named accountable owner.

    Review outcomes, exceptions, release responsibilities and audit evidence must remain visible until the appropriate decision.

SPECIALIST PROGRAMMES

Complexity deserves precision.

Explore how evidence requirements change across pharmaceutical presentations. These are assessment categories, not a product catalogue or availability statement.

01Oral solid programmes

The familiar presentation still requires exact evidence.

Strength, pack, source product, shelf life, labelling, repackaging, storage and the applicable licence pathway must be established product by product.

  • Identity and reference-product evidence
  • Manufacturer and supply rights
  • Pack, stability and storage requirements
  • Applicable authorisation pathway
02Oral liquid programmes

Formulation determines the pathway.

Concentration, excipients, closure compatibility, microbiological controls, in-use conditions and technology-transfer requirements can change both the evidence and the accountable parties.

  • Formula and development rationale
  • Container-closure and analytical strategy
  • Microbiology, stability and in-use evidence
  • Manufacturing and transfer responsibilities
03Sterile and temperature-sensitive

Specialist controls need specialist owners.

Where applicable, qualified manufacturers, laboratories, packaging systems, release arrangements and logistics providers must demonstrate the required control architecture.

  • Authorised specialist organisations
  • Sterility and release evidence
  • Qualified packaging and transport lanes
  • Temperature monitoring and excursion review
04Specialist and supportive care

Clinical significance does not create commercial authority.

A substantiated B2B need is only the starting point. Product rights, available evidence, regulated route, quality requirements and permissible claims require separate assessment.

  • Documented B2B need
  • Product and supplier authority
  • Quality, handling and commercial boundaries
  • Approved communication and intended audience

QUESTIONS & SCOPE

Clarity matters.

Current operating boundaries and practical questions for prospective partners.

Does NovaPharm currently sell oncology medicines?

No. Oncology and specialist medicines are strategic areas for B2B opportunity assessment. This page does not state that any oncology medicine is approved, stocked or available from NovaPharm. Regulated wholesale activity will begin only after the required authorisations are granted.

Is NovaPharm an MHRA-authorised pharmaceutical wholesaler?

NovaPharm does not present itself as holding a WDA(H) or another unverified pharmaceutical authorisation. Company incorporation and preparatory work are separate from permissions required for regulated supply.

Can patients use this page to choose or order medicines?

No. This is a corporate B2B page. NovaPharm does not provide diagnosis, treatment, dosage, medicine-selection or prescribing advice and does not accept patient orders.

What does oncology continuity mean here?

It means connecting product identity, evidence, source, condition, demand assumptions and accountable decisions so gaps are visible before supply or release decisions. It is a governance framework, not a guarantee of availability.

Does NovaPharm operate its own cold chain, laboratory or manufacturing site?

No such ownership is claimed. Product-specific manufacture, testing, release, storage and transport would require appropriately qualified and authorised organisations with explicit responsibilities and evidence.

How can a product owner or qualified partner begin a discussion?

Start with a non-confidential description of the opportunity through the business enquiry route. Do not send patient information, adverse-event reports, urgent medical details or a confidential dossier through the public contact page.
