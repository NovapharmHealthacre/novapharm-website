    DESIGNED FOR TRUST

    Every answer should show
    what kind of answer it is.

    Our evidence model separates what has been observed from what has been calculated, forecast, assumed or recommended.

    01Observed fact

    What a named, dated source actually reports.

    02Derived metric

    What a transparent and reproducible method calculates.

    03Model forecast

    What a specified model predicts, with uncertainty and validation.

    04Commercial assumption

    What a user or business scenario elects to assume.

    05Recommendation

    What a decision-support process proposes for human assessment.

    Built around source provenance, freshness, jurisdiction, role-based access, explicit uncertainty and human review. An unresolved match stays unresolved.
