Vishal is available for selected speaking, editorial and partnership conversations on building pharmaceutical companies and taking products into regulated markets.

## Speaking themes

- **Market access:** connect approval, manufacturing, supply, price and channel adoption.
- **UK–EU entry:** design for connected markets with distinct regulatory and commercial systems.
- **CMO/CDMO strategy:** assess technical capability, documentation, batch economics and lifecycle fit.
- **Technology transfer:** align product knowledge, analytical methods, equipment, validation and economics.
- **Supply resilience:** manage supplier concentration, capacity, lead time, inventory and contingency routes.
- **Specialist medicines:** build reliable access for focused products and smaller patient populations.
- **Founder execution:** connect product strategy, specialist advice, capital and operating decisions.

## Editorial and roundtable formats

Relevant formats include interviews, panels, private roundtables, written commentary, editorial collaboration and workshops. Topics may include pharmaceutical strategy, manufacturing partnerships, portfolio selection, working capital, digital operations and cross-border company building.

## Partnership conversations

Selected discussions may cover product and market-access strategy, manufacturing or technology-transfer opportunities, distribution, channel development, research exchange and editorial collaboration.
