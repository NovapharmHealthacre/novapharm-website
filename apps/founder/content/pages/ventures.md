## What NovaPharm is building

### Product and market access

Product opportunities are assessed through market need, regulatory route, manufacturing feasibility, batch economics, channel fit and lifecycle value.

### Licensing and manufacturing

Regulatory planning is connected to product selection, development scope, manufacturing, packaging and launch. CMO/CDMO relationships are evaluated for technical capability, documentation, batch size, capacity and commercial fit.

### Supply and commercialisation

The model prioritises qualified sources, clear responsibilities, realistic lead times, inventory visibility and resilient channels. Supply continuity is designed into the product strategy rather than added after launch.

### Digital operating infrastructure

NovaPharm is designing a governed data layer across product, partner, inventory, quality and commercial information to improve traceability and decision visibility.

## Direction

The United Kingdom is the strategic foundation. Europe, India and selected regulated markets form part of the longer-term product and partnership direction. Expansion is intended to follow verified capability, not geography alone.

## Founder role

Vishal leads company strategy, product selection, manufacturing and commercial partnerships, market-access planning and development of the operating platform.
