Target markets. Local responsibility.

Five regions. Five different pathways.

Product-specific opportunities. Local permissions first.

01   United Kingdom

Our home market.

Building regulatory readiness and qualified supply pathways. Required MHRA authorisations and GDP operating controls must precede regulated supply.

02   European Union

Selected partnerships.

Exploring member-state opportunities through locally authorised partners. UK wholesale permissions do not transfer to EU member states.

03   United States

Local pathways.

Evaluating state-specific licensing and commercial partnerships. Applicable state licences and DSCSA requirements govern prescription wholesale activities.

04   Canada

Product-specific market routes.

Assessing importer, distributor and product-specific routes. Drug, device and natural-health-product requirements remain distinct.

05   Middle East & North Africa

Regional opportunity assessment.

Considering country-specific partnerships, including the UAE and Saudi Arabia. Establishment, import and distribution permissions require local assessment.

These are markets under evaluation, not claims of active regulated pharmaceutical distribution or existing wholesale authorisations across these territories.

Expansion discipline

Local permissions.
Shared standards.

For each target market, we assess demand, regulatory requirements, authorised counterparties, commercial viability, product evidence and accountable supply arrangements.

Current regulatory boundary. Regulated pharmaceutical wholesale supply has not commenced. Any future supply depends on required local authorisations, product-specific permissions, qualified suppliers, GDP/quality controls and permitted buyer channels. UK permissions do not confer distribution rights in other countries.
