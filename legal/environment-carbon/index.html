01

Applicability and measurement

No verified company data currently show that NovaPharm meets the applicable large-company tests. Group position, size, energy use and procurement requirements will be reviewed with the accounts process. Operational emissions measurement and a defensible baseline remain in development.

02

Priority sources

Materiality review will consider contracted warehousing, temperature-controlled and international freight, purchased logistics, packaging, waste, business travel, digital infrastructure, supplier energy and manufacturing evidence.

  • Scope 1 direct sources
  • Scope 2 purchased electricity
  • Material Scope 3 logistics, travel, packaging and suppliers
03

Claims boundary

NovaPharm does not claim carbon neutrality, net zero, zero emissions, science-based targets, carbon-negative operation or verified reductions. Any future figure will state period, boundary, method, factors, intensity measure, limitations and review status.
