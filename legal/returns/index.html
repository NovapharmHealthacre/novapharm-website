1. Your seller and how to contact us

Your seller is NovaPharm Healthcare Ltd (registered in England and Wales, company 16716501), registered office: Flat 72 Archer Court, 21 High Street, Feltham, England, TW13 4AG, United Kingdom. For Nutraxin orders, delivery concerns, cancellation, complaints or returns, email support@novapharmhealthcare.com with your order reference. Please do not send card numbers or other payment credentials.

2. UK delivery

Only UK consumer delivery is currently offered in this Nutraxin retail journey. Standard UK delivery for Nutraxin is £4.49 per eligible order, shown separately before payment. The delivery destination, final price and any additional services are shown before payment. Delivery is arranged through our fulfilment partners, and we remain responsible as the seller.

We will deliver without undue delay and, unless an alternative delivery period is expressly agreed with you, no later than 30 days after the contract is made. We do not promise next-day dispatch or a particular carrier transit time unless a specific commitment is shown and accepted at checkout. Contact support with your reference if delivery is overdue, missing or damaged.

3. Cancelling an eligible online purchase

You normally have 14 days after receiving your goods to tell us you wish to cancel a UK distance purchase without giving a reason. Contact support with your order number and a clear statement of cancellation. You then have a further 14 days after informing us to send the goods back. This is in addition to your separate rights if products are faulty or misdescribed.

For ordinary change-of-mind returns, you are responsible for reasonable direct return postage costs where the law permits and this was disclosed before purchase. Please contact us for the correct, accepted UK returns address and routing instructions before posting goods. The registered office is for legal correspondence, not for unsolicited product returns. A fulfilment warehouse may not be able to receive unannounced parcels.

4. Sealed supplements and hygiene

Some sealed goods may be exempt from the change-of-mind cancellation right if, after delivery, the seal is broken and the goods genuinely cannot be returned for health protection or hygiene reasons. We apply any exception only where the law permits, not as a blanket refusal of all opened packaging. Opening a shipping carton is not automatically the same as breaking a product's hygiene seal. None of these exceptions limits your rights for faulty, unsafe or misdescribed goods.

5. Damaged, incorrect or faulty items

If goods arrive damaged, incorrect, unsafe or not as described, contact our support team promptly with the order reference and any relevant photos of the packaging. We will arrange an appropriate remedy and, where required by law, reasonable return costs at our expense. Customers generally have a short-term right to reject faulty goods within 30 days, with additional statutory remedies after that. No informal reporting target removes those rights.

6. Refunds

For an eligible cancellation, refunds include the price paid and any eligible original standard delivery charge. We will refund within 14 days after receiving the returned goods or acceptable evidence that you sent them, using the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise. We may make only deductions allowed by law for handling beyond what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics and functioning of goods. For faulty goods, applicable statutory remedies and refund rules take priority.

7. How to request a return

Email support@novapharmhealthcare.com, stating your order number, the goods concerned, whether you are cancelling or reporting a fault and how we can contact you. We will provide the suitable UK returns destination and instructions. Keep proof of postage and, where available, tracking. Please do not send parcels to an unconfirmed site.

8. Cancellation form (optional)

You may email the following statement or use your own clear wording: “I hereby give notice that I cancel my contract for the following Nutraxin goods: [product and quantity]. Ordered on [date], received on [date]. Customer name, order reference and contact details: [details].”

9. Your rights

This information is intended to reflect applicable UK consumer law, including the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 and Consumer Rights Act 2015. Nothing here excludes or reduces mandatory consumer rights. For other matters, see our website terms and privacy notice.
