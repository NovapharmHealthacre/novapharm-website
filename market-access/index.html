A clear sequence

Six questions before launch.

Regulatory approval, commercial market access and physical supply are distinct decisions.

  1. 01

    Product identity

    What is the formulation, dossier, presentation, rights holder and current authorisation status?

  2. 02

    Country and route

    Which local registration, import, wholesale, device or supplement regime applies?

  3. 03

    Accountability

    Who is responsible for regulatory ownership, quality, importation and contractual duties?

  4. 04

    Commercial viability

    What are the relevant procurement channels, reimbursement conditions, demand and economics?

  5. 05

    Supply integrity

    Which sources, quality agreements, GDP controls, handling records and continuity plans are required?

  6. 06

    Release readiness

    Have the applicable authorisations, evidence and customer eligibility been independently verified?

Current regulatory boundary

Regulated pharmaceutical wholesale supply has not commenced. Any future supply depends on required local authorisations, product-specific permissions, qualified suppliers, GDP/quality controls and permitted buyer channels. UK permissions do not confer distribution rights in other countries.

Commercial applications

From the dossier to the decision.

Opportunities may involve generics, oncology, specialist medicines, OTC, PLPI strategy, medical devices or food supplements. Each category requires its own regulatory and commercial assessment.

NovaPharm Healthcare is active in corporate, product, partnership and commercial-development work. Regulated wholesale supply has not commenced and will begin only after the required MHRA authorisations and applicable operating controls are in place. Product-specific parallel-import activity remains subject to the grant and maintenance of the relevant PLPI licence.

Pharmaceutical services, medicine sourcing and regulated supply programmes are intended for qualified business audiences and remain subject to the required authorisations. Nutraxin food supplements have a separate UK consumer shopping journey, subject to product information and order terms. NovaPharm does not provide medical advice or sell prescription medicines directly to patients. Seek advice from a healthcare professional for medical concerns.
