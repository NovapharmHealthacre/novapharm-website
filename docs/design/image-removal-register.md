# Corporate image removal register

Review date: 22 August 2026
Scope: NovaPharm Healthcare public corporate experience

## Decision rule

An image remains only when it depicts an approved person or product, materially explains the subject, and can be presented without implying an unverified NovaPharm facility, licence, inventory, clinical operation or scale. Decorative diagrams, molecular scenes, pseudo-platform visuals and stock-photo mosaics are removed from the active corporate composition. Legacy source assets may remain in the repository for traceable rollback, but are not evidence of current use.

| Route or family | Previous treatment | Decision | Current treatment | Truth boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | Multi-panel supply and scientific imagery with equal visual weight | Reduce | One restrained hero treatment, typographic sourcing routes, one real Nutraxin packshot and selective supporting photography | Images never assert current inventory, owned facilities or regulated supply |
| About and Company | Large media boxes and repeated corporate scenes | Reduce | One relevant editorial image where it helps; legal identity and operating principles lead | Captions identify representative business context |
| Capabilities and Services | Decorative service images and feature mosaics | Remove from core service explanation | Text-led capability ledger and operating sequence | Capability is described through scope and evidence, not imagery |
| Regulatory & Quality | Abstract process graphics and small decorative markers | Remove | Seven-stage numbered regulatory roadmap plus one realistic batch-document image | The page states that WDA(H), PLPI and permission to supply are not currently granted |
| CRO | Programme diagrams and conceptual architecture imagery | Reduce | Text-led responsibility model with one evidence-review photograph | NovaPharm is not presented as a full-service CRO or sponsor |
| Oncology | Oversized scientific and decorative gallery treatment | Remove | Controlled editorial composition with at most one relevant vial-handling image | Caption states the image is representative and not a NovaPharm facility, product or active programme |
| Products | Mixed generic pharmaceutical imagery | Separate | Approved Nutraxin product packshots lead; strategic portfolio is text-led | No current stock, availability, price, licence or commerce claim |
| Nutraxin overview and details | Generic category art | Remove | Approved catalogue-derived product pack images only | No pack image is redrawn or AI-generated |
| Partners | Stock-photo mosaics and handshake-style pathways | Remove | Qualification criteria and partner routes presented editorially | No unverified partner logo or relationship is shown |
| Technology | Faux dashboards, server art and maturity theatre | Remove | Text-led control architecture and explicit maturity labels | Synthetic metrics are not shown as operational proof |
| Insights | Six equal-weight image cards | Reduce | Lead analysis, secondary stories and a clean article list; imagery optional | Editorial priority is not inferred from decorative imagery |
| Leadership | Mixed or generated portraits | Replace with approved owner-supplied portraits where available | Governed responsive portrait derivatives with consistent crop rules | Portrait identity and title records are checksum-controlled |

## Active-media acceptance

- Core transformed markup contains no decorative SVG scene, icon-feature grid, molecular drawing, DNA art or pseudo-3D architecture illustration.
- The official NovaPharm identity SVG remains permitted.
- Approved product and leadership media remain permitted.
- Every retained representative image has meaningful alternative text and, where interpretation could be misleading, a visible disclosure caption.
- Browser acceptance must verify focal points at mobile and desktop widths before final approval.

## Deliberate non-deletion

Legacy media is not bulk-deleted in this branch because it is referenced by historical evidence, rollback artefacts or non-corporate applications. The release test governs active references rather than pretending archival files are current presentation assets.
