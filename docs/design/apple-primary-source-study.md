proprietary fonts, source code, trade dress or page compositions. NovaPharm
must remain an original, regulated pharmaceutical experience.

## Eight principles translated for NovaPharm

### Purpose

Every page, section, field, metric and Portal module must serve a known visitor
or operator need. The medicine, product, decision or task is the subject. A
surface without a distinct job is removed, combined or moved deeper.

### Agency

People control navigation, filtering, disclosure, motion and submission. They
can escape drawers and guided flows, recover from errors and understand what
will happen next. No scroll-jacking, forced narrative or false success state is
permitted.

### Responsibility

Regulatory state, product classification, data provenance, forecast
uncertainty, privacy, account qualification, role authority and production
maturity remain explicit at the point of use. NovaPharm collects the minimum
necessary data and never implies licences, supply, commerce, dispensing,
clinical authority or live operation without evidence.

### Familiarity

Controls that look alike behave alike. Links navigate, buttons execute,
disclosures reveal and fields retain conventional labels. Placement and
language remain stable across public, Portal and PharmaScope surfaces so people
can transfer what they learn.

### Flexibility

Every principal experience adapts to mobile, tablet, desktop, zoom, text
resize, keyboard, touch, reduced motion, forced colours and both Chromium and
WebKit. Adaptation preserves task context; it does not merely shrink a desktop
layout.

### Simplicity

Simplicity is exactly enough, not visual emptiness. Remove repetition,
decorative UI and unnecessary steps while retaining the evidence, filters,
warnings and recovery information needed for an informed pharmaceutical
decision.

### Craft

Craft includes typography, line length, image crop, alignment, table density,
focus geometry, loading and empty states, source truth, fast queries, stable
layout, secure boundaries and repeatable builds. It is proven by iteration and
exact-tree testing, not by describing a surface as premium.

### Delight

The appropriate emotion is calm confidence. Delight comes from immediate
search, coherent context, truthful feedback, predictable recovery and precise
visual execution. Decorative effects, copied motifs and gratuitous motion are
not substitutes.

## Current public-pattern findings

- The Apple UK homepage still demonstrates identity, concise proposition,
  limited actions and the real product as focal point.
- Current family pages use local navigation and progressive depth; global
  navigation does not carry every supporting route.
- Current purchase flows expose real choice, price and availability only where
  commerce is authoritative. NovaPharm's current no-price state is therefore a
  deliberate truth boundary, not an unfinished purchase screen.
- Business and healthcare pages move from broad value to practical capability,
  security and a real next action.
- Privacy, security and accessibility are part of the product story rather
  than legal-page afterthoughts.
- Contact paths are intent-specific. A single generic form is insufficient for
  sales, support, compliance, media and account qualification.

## Candidate review conclusion

The current NovaPharm candidate already follows the applicable structural
lessons: seven-destination global navigation, scoped local navigation,
editorial public pages, pack-led Nutraxin routes, truthful commerce absence,
topic-led managed forms, a fail-closed public Portal boundary and a protected,
text-first PharmaScope workspace. The 2026 refresh does not justify returning
to the earlier decorative or card-heavy architecture.

The fresh review did identify one Portal craft defect: the Medicines
Intelligence banner retained an empty-source label after accepted analytical
facts loaded. The banner is now query-aware, and browser acceptance fails if
the stale label reappears. No other material repository-controlled visual
restructure was justified by the current primary sources.

## Explicit non-copying rules

- No Apple logos, product imagery, icons, proprietary font files or source.
- No pixel-for-pixel recreation of an Apple page or identifiable trade dress.
- No public claim that NovaPharm is Apple-designed, Apple-certified or
  Apple-grade.
- No assertion about private Apple implementation details.
- The benchmark is limited to general disciplines: purpose, restraint,
  hierarchy, responsiveness, accessibility, performance, truth and craft.
