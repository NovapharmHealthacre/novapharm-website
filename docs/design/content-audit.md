## Writing controls

- Active voice and precise verbs replace broad claims such as "transform", "revolutionise" and "world-leading".
- "Live", "repository complete", "external verification pending" and "planned" are distinct terms.
- "Enquiry", "interest", "application", "approval", "portal identity" and "regulated release" remain distinct lifecycle states.
- No public sentence states or implies a current MHRA WDA(H), PLPI licence, current regulated wholesale activity, current NHS commercial supply, product availability or NovaPharm-owned manufacturing facility.
- Required regulatory, safety, privacy and medical boundaries remain visible even when long brochure copy is removed.
