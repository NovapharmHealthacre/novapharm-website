## SOP-D01 | Licence scope and regulatory role determination

**Purpose:** Establish whether the proposed activity is wholesale dealing, independent brokering, importation, PLPI, manufacturing/assembly, or non-medicine distribution. The licence must match the legal entity, site and regulated activity.

**Responsible:** Regulatory lead; independent legal/quality reviewer; RP where required.

**Process:**
1. Open a unique opportunity record with product identity, intended actors, destination, channel and whether NovaPharm takes title, negotiates, imports, stores, supplies or handles.
2. Identify current UK law/MHRA requirements, separately for the actual product category. Record source links and regulator advice where needed.
3. Verify the current granted licence/registration in the official regulator register and original document, including conditions, sites, named persons, activities, dates and variations. A pending application is not an authorisation.
4. List counterparties' separate rights and licenses. A logistics partner's authority does not grant NovaPharm wholesale rights.
5. Sign the route matrix before any medicine offer, order, transfer or shipment. Reassess after changes to actors, destinations or authorisations.

**Records:** D01 route matrix; licence copies; register-check receipt; approvals; variation/change history. **Stop conditions:** missing/expired licence, unapproved activity, unresolved exemption, unknown legal role.

## SOP-D02 | Quality management, roles and change control

**Purpose:** Put documented GDP controls under accountable quality oversight.

**Responsible:** Quality lead/RP (if applicable), operations, outsourced service owners.

**Process:** Define and approve the quality manual and organisational chart; assign independent release and escalation authority; record training; establish controlled document numbering; require approvals and risk assessments for process, facility, carrier, IT and supply changes. Maintain periodic self-inspections and corrective/preventive actions. Ensure staff cannot override quality blocks or self-approve high-risk decisions.

**Records:** training matrix; controlled SOP list; change requests; deviation/CAPA log; self-inspections; management-review minutes. **Stop conditions:** missing RP/quality coverage, missing current SOP, unauthorised change.

## SOP-D03 | Supplier onboarding and periodic requalification

**Responsible:** Procurement and independent QA.

1. Confirm company registration, full legal details, site, beneficial customer/supplier role and contact channel.
2. Check the current medicine/manufacturer/wholesale/import permissions and product-specific source chain using trusted registers and original documents.
3. Record medicine origin, procurement route, authorised supply scope, product-rights evidence and authenticity controls.
4. Approve a commercial agreement and a separate quality/technical agreement where applicable.
5. Risk-rank suppliers and require documented periodic checks; suspend for expiry, counterfeit alerts, recalls or unexplained supply chain changes.

**Records:** D06 supplier master, licence check, risk assessment, audit/QTA receipt. **No supply:** pending, invalid, unreviewed or suspended supplier.

## SOP-D04 | Customer onboarding and permitted B2B channels

**Responsible:** Commercial initiates; QA verifies; Finance approves credit.

1. Verify legal identity, shipping location and eligibility for the exact medicine class and market, including customer licence where required.
2. Separately verify destination/import permissions and product channel restrictions.
3. Record current official licence reference, reviewer, effective date and revalidation interval.
4. Restrict access and offerings to permitted approved SKUs and geography; keep anonymous contacts as enquiries, never order-ready customers.
5. Suspend eligibility upon expiration, material change, sanctions flags, recall, quality or financial hold.

**Records:** D07 buyer register and reviewer history. **No release:** a contact form submission or unchecked account is not a buyer qualification.

## SOP-D05 | Product master, territorial rights and authorisations

**Responsible:** Regulatory/Product lead; QA sign-off.

Record canonical product identity, active substance, strength, formulation, presentation/pack, MA/PLPI or other legally applicable pathway, holder, valid markets, serialisation requirements, import restrictions, label language and manufacturer/source chain. Separate licensed medicines, unlicensed routes, PLPI, food supplements and devices; do not assume one category authorisation transfers to another. Retain supporting documents and verified revision history.

**Stop conditions:** ambiguous identity/pack, missing marketing/PLPI rights, destination ineligibility, unauthorised claim, unqualified origin.

## SOP-D06 | Storage premises, security and equipment qualification

**Responsible:** Quality and warehouse/site operator.

Approve a written site scope; complete physical security and access assessment, facility environmental qualification, pest control, segregation of quarantine/rejects/returns/recalls, maintenance and documented calibration. For outsourced premises require an approved quality agreement, audit/qualification evidence, service levels and responsibility split. The warehouse photograph on the website is not proof of site qualification.

**Records:** facility audit, approved site list, warehouse map, equipment calibration records, security incidents, outsourced agreements.

## SOP-D07 | Temperature handling, mapping and deviations

**Responsible:** Quality/RP and validated warehouse/carrier operators.

1. Read the product-specific label and stability requirements; distinguish controlled ambient from refrigerated handling.
2. Qualify each actual warehouse zone and each intended carrier lane for the applicable range before use.
3. Identify calibrated logger IDs, mapping dates, monitoring frequency, alarms, contingency response, data retention and owner.
4. For temperature excursions immediately quarantine affected stock, preserve raw logger evidence, open a deviation and request formal product/quality disposition. **Never automatically release** from a generic 2–8°C or 15–25°C website description.
5. Escalate unresolved excursions to QA/RP and source holder when required.

**Records:** site/lane qualification; temperature logs; alarm and deviation records; risk assessment; disposition decision.

## SOP-D08 | Receipt, batch/expiry inventory and quarantine

**Responsible:** Receiving operator; QA accepts.

Record supplier, purchase reference, product and exact pack, quantity, batch/lot, expiry, source and transport identity, receipt timestamp, package integrity, tampering/security seals, serialisation where applicable, transport temperature and original documents. Record stock as **quarantined** until identity, chain, temperature and authorised source checks pass. Apply FEFO and document each movement with immutable actor/time references. No assumed inventory or negative stock.

**Records:** goods-received note, stock movements, batch ledger, release/quarantine receipts, discrepancy investigations.

## SOP-D09 | Order and finance review

**Responsible:** Commercial/Finance; independent QA release remains mandatory.

A request begins as an enquiry/quote, not a medicine sale. Before offer or commitment, verify current entity/product/market permissions, counterparty qualification, approved stock, terms, tax/VAT basis, price approval, credit/payment authorisation and contractual compliance. Keep medicine ordering separate from Nutraxin food-supplement and ESCO equipment checkout. Deny transaction creation if any gate is unverified; do not charge a card for an unapproved medicinal order.

**Records:** qualified quote, approval trail, invoice and ledger entries, order revisions and exceptions.

## SOP-D10 | Quality disposition and dispatch

**Responsible:** Trained warehouse team; independent designated QA/RP as required.

Check current licences, site and lane qualification, exact SKU rights, approved buyer/supplier, released in-date batch, FEFO, stock quantity, no open quality/recall/temperature hold, and packaging/traceability. Record independent disposition. Only then pick, verify and dispatch by qualified carrier with chain of custody, calibrated logger where applicable, dispatch manifest, track-and-trace and signed proof of delivery. The application must fail closed after approval expiry or any new hold.

**Records:** signed disposition, pack/pick check, dispatch note, carrier handover, POD and shipping condition record.

## SOP-D11 | Returns, complaints and falsified medicines

**Responsible:** Quality lead and authorised warehouse personnel.

Segregate all returns immediately; retain original conditions, receipt, batch/serial, source, chain-of-custody and any product complaint. Do not return goods to saleable stock unless a documented applicable GDP process and authorised reviewer approve. Investigate any suspected falsification or integrity breach with escalation to the proper authorities and marketing authorisation holder as applicable.

**Records:** return authorisation, quality decision, complaint investigation, recall linkage and CAPA.

## SOP-D12 | Product recalls and mock recall

**Responsible:** Quality/RP; Commercial and Logistics execute approved instructions.

Maintain a current out-of-hours escalation roster (not published), batch traceability and supplier/customer contact map. Upon recall/quality alert: place affected lots and offers on immediate hold, identify all movements and affected counterparties, follow authority and MAH requirements, manage returns/destruction where authorised, reconcile affected quantities and document communications. Rehearse a **synthetic** batch recall before activation and periodically afterwards, with measured completeness and corrective actions.

**Records:** recall decision, audit trail, reconciliation, mock recall timings, independent sign-off.

## SOP-D13 | Incidents, CAPA and self-inspection

**Responsible:** Quality/Responsible Person where applicable.

Every incident obtains a timestamped ID and severity, batch/site/carrier associations, containment and product risk decision. Root cause and corrective/preventive actions are documented, assigned, independently verified for effectiveness and closed with approval. Conduct periodic risk-based GDP self-inspections; maintain an audit calendar and escalation triggers.

## SOP-D14 | Privacy, system access, backups and audit trail

**Responsible:** Technology/security lead and Quality.

Grant role-based least-privilege permissions, separate development/staging/production, restrict edit and release rights, capture audit events for each quality-sensitive action, use encryption and managed secrets, retention policy, regular restore tests and incident response. Protect personal/business contact data. Require two-person review for privileged overrides and forbid direct browser-set status flips. A test fixture or AI-generated record can never satisfy production evidence.

## SOP-D15 | End-to-end readiness and management approval

**Responsible:** Independent Quality/RP, Regulatory, Finance and accountable company signatory.

Run witnessed rehearsals on safe synthetic records: enquiry → buyer/source qualification → product/country rights → goods receipt/quarantine → batch release → quote/finance → approved lane → traceable dispatch → POD → return/complaint/recall → reconciliation. Test rejected licence, wrong country, buyer restriction, missing MA/PLPI, stock shortfall, expired/quarantined lot, unresolved excursion, carrier failure and recall hold. Produce signed, timestamped test results with exact environment/version references. Production remains locked until all required approvals are independently accepted.

## Controlled record templates: required columns

These are **schemas, not real records**. No operational status is asserted.

| Record | Minimum fields |
| --- | --- |
| Distribution activity | Legal entity ID, jurisdiction, activity/role, required authorisation, site ID, valid-from/to, original evidence hash, independent reviewer |
| Supplier and customer | Actor ID, legal name, role, permitted channels, licence/registration scope, verification authority, review and expiry date, approval status |
| Product right | Medicine identity, pack, MA/PLPI reference, rights holder, territory, handling conditions, licence dates, reviewer |
| Facility and lane | Site/carrier/lane IDs, contract and QTA references, GDP/site scope, mapping/calibration/validation evidence, dates |
| Lot inventory | SKU, batch, expiry, owner, supplier, site/bin, quantity/unit, receipt, quarantine/disposition, movement IDs |
| Temperature event | Product/batch, calibrated logger, condition range, observed timestamps and values, excursion, containment and QA disposition |
| Commercial order | Qualified buyer, market, SKU, price/tax and terms, QA release ID, finance approval, status, immutable actor timestamps |
| Shipment and POD | Order, lot and carrier, lane, packaging/logger, custody handoffs, tracking, receipt and exceptions |
| Complaint/recall | Source, product/batch, affected quantities/customers, containment, root cause, authority/MAH escalation, reconciliation and CAPA |
| Release certificate | Exact gate D01–D15 evidence IDs, independent reviewer roles, status, signature, expiry, immutable revision and deployment |

**Evidence retention and SOP revision periods:** To be set by the accountable quality/legal owners under the applicable law and approved records policy, not invented in this draft.
