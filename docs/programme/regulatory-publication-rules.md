# Regulatory Publication Rules

Status: repository rules implemented; qualified and legal review remains item-specific
Review date: 1 August 2026
Owners: Quality and Regulatory, Legal, Content Governance

## Release rule

A regulated or operational claim may publish only when its exact visible wording, subject, holder, jurisdiction, scope, date and evidence state are approved. The same decision applies to metadata, structured data, search indexes, feeds, portal labels and downloadable materials.

## Prohibited without exact evidence

- NovaPharm WDA(H), PLPI, MHRA approval or GDP certificate claims.
- Current NHS supply, product availability, licensed inventory or commercial revenue claims.
- NovaPharm-owned manufacturing, warehouse or logistics-facility claims.
- Transfer of a partner's licence, certificate or authorisation to NovaPharm.
- Operational AI, blockchain, pharmacovigilance, finance, WMS or Microsoft 365 capability where only architecture exists.
- Prescription-product promotion, patient ordering, efficacy or prescribing guidance.

## Required qualifiers

Use `planned`, `in development`, `target market`, `through qualified partners` or `subject to applicable authorisation` only when those states are accurate. A qualifier must be adjacent and understandable; it cannot be hidden in a footer while a headline implies current operation.

## People and appointments

Executive title, governance fact and regulated appointment are separate fields. Dr Nishita Trivedi's owner-approved public title is `Chief Technology Officer and Responsible Person`: Chief Technology Officer is the executive responsibility and Responsible Person is the distinct regulated appointment. Formal appointment documentation remains controlled by `owner-evidence-request-dr-nishita-responsible-person.md`. The title must not be expanded into a claim that she acts for the MHRA, grants approvals, holds unevidenced statutory powers, or has prescribing, medical or clinical authority. She is not a statutory director.

## Partner and certificate wording

Owner-attested relationships may be described at that level. Certificate-specific statements require exact holder, number, site, scope, effective date, restrictions and current register status. The Polar Speed boundary is defined in `operating-status-and-logistics-evidence.md`.

## Automation

The claims package and cross-site tests reject expired/current regulated claims without acceptable evidence. Automation is a publication control, not legal or regulatory approval. Human reviewers remain accountable for evidence quality and wording.
