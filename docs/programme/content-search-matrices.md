## Competitor and search landscape

The benchmark in `seo/category-benchmark.md` covers major pharma, CDMO and logistics expectations. NovaPharm does not compete on multinational scale or owned infrastructure. Its defensible topic territory is the integration of market access, regulatory sequencing, product economics, qualified sourcing, technical transfer and evidence integrity.

Verified search demand and rankings are unavailable until Search Console/Bing ownership is active. Therefore the repository uses stakeholder language and primary-source topic validation rather than fabricated volume figures.

## Regulatory-content matrix

| Topic | Allowed public treatment | Required source/review | Prohibited implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| WDA(H) | Readiness, responsibilities and authorisation dependency | MHRA/GOV.UK plus qualified review | NovaPharm currently holds WDA(H) |
| PLPI | Assessment framework and market context | MHRA/legislation plus regulatory review | Approved or available NovaPharm PLPI products |
| GDP/QMS | Governance principles and readiness | Official GDP/MHRA sources plus Quality review | Certified or 100% compliant without evidence |
| Pharmacovigilance | Readiness and controlled-route need | MHRA/legislation plus qualified safety owner | Operational PV system without connection/owner |
| Oncology/specialty | B2B supply/forecasting considerations | Primary sources and technical review | Patient promotion, treatment or availability |
| Logistics/Polar Speed | Owner-attested contracted relationship and control model | Controlled agreements and approved wording | Partner certificate belongs to NovaPharm |
| AI/blockchain | Governance and architecture | System card/risk register/security review | Deployed automated decisions or blockchain traceability |

## Refresh schedule

- Monthly: broken sources, redirects, indexability and urgent factual changes.
- Quarterly: crawler guidance, key corporate facts, external profiles, priority Insights and search-platform data.
- Six-monthly: service/regulatory hubs, content gaps and internal links.
- Annually: strategy, competitor benchmark, legal policies, executive biographies and full entity audit.
- Event-driven: authorisation, appointment, material partnership, regulation, product-state or security change.

Cosmetic builds do not change editorial dates.
