**Research date:** 8 November 2026. **Purpose:** NovaPharm UK B2B pricing research, not authorised selling prices.

**Evidence boundary:** Direct live Amazon.co.uk cart prices could not be independently verified through the accessible search channels. The prices below are quoted from UK comparison services attributing products to **Amazon Marketplace** or Amazon-linked offers, sometimes with older caches. Treat all observations as **indicative and unverified at checkout**, not Amazon's own retail price, not NovaPharm's trading price, and not evidence of availability or stock. Amazon may be a separate contractual carve-out from NovaPharm's UK distribution appointment; validate commercial rights.

## Commercial decision gate

It must not be imported into `price_lists`, `price_list_items`, or public Product `Offer` schema without an independent, owner-approved commercial decision. Preserve the existing fail-closed Nutraxin order submission restriction.

Required for each of 19 SKUs: GTIN/EAN exact product identity, approved UK pack and compliant food-supplement claims; current Amazon seller, time and promotion status; NovaPharm's actual COGS/landed cost; account-specific GBP **ex-VAT** price if VAT-registered and VAT treatment confirmed; minimum order quantity, payment terms and delivery charge; warehouse available-to-promise and product release; effective dates and approval sign-off.
