# NovaPharm founder-led marketing | zero-paid-media release standard

**Owner direction:** Steve Jobs-inspired product storytelling: one idea per page, show the actual product, remove friction, and ensure the experience fulfils the promise. Do not copy Apple's designs, marks, advertising, or fonts.

## Audience and truthful promise

| Brand | Hero message | Primary conversion | Claims boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NovaPharm Healthcare | Medicine. Where it needs to be. | Qualified B2B partnership enquiry | Planned distribution is not represented as authorised supply |
| Nutraxin | Meet Nutraxin. / Wellness, without the noise. | Browse Products, add to Bag and follow on-site checkout | Do not claim successful live payment or fulfillment until production webhook, order records and shipping are independently verified |
| NovaPharm Intelligence | Medicine is complex. Understanding it shouldn't be. | Explore a clearly marked development-stage platform vision | Simulated interfaces and unshipped features remain labelled |

## Product-led page contract

1. One clear headline, visually prioritising the product and supporting evidence.
2. A concise purpose statement and one primary action that works today.
3. Secondary actions only where useful; preserve keyboard and mobile accessibility.
4. Maintain authorisation, supplier and development-stage disclosures.
5. No marketing pixels, cookie-based trackers, paid promotions or AI-generated health claims without controls.
6. Preserve the Products-only checkout implementation, but only claim proven payment availability after prices, checkout, fulfilment and legal eligibility have been verified in production. Merchant offer/availability markup must reflect real customer orderability.
7. Use only owned assets and platform-native system fonts.

## Free tools, after verified setup

- **Google Search Console:** verify property, submit sitemaps, monitor indexing and organic search. Requires site-owner access.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools:** verify property, submit sitemaps, examine IndexNow and available AI-search citations.
- **Google Analytics 4:** establish GA4 and conversions only after appropriate cookie consent and privacy assessment. Paid-order events must come from verified backend state, not button clicks.
- **Microsoft Clarity:** require privacy review and masking; never record sensitive forms, health data, credentials, checkout or portal screens.
- **Google Merchant Center free listings:** after independent verification of the new Nutraxin Products-only checkout, submit only eligible products with approved descriptions and compliant shipping/pricing data.
- **LinkedIn Company Pages and YouTube:** original, reviewed educational content and product demonstrations; no paid media.
- **Looker Studio:** free core executive dashboard; distinguish unique visitors, qualified leads and completed orders.

Do not put marketing IDs, API keys or customer information in the repository.

## Initial organic editorial programme

- Nutraxin: formulation explainer, pack-size guide, range navigation, factual product FAQ.
- Healthcare: UK market access, GDP controls, supplier qualification, authorisation versus planned activity.
- Intelligence: medicine identity, evidence versus forecast, geography and market context, illustrative Medicines Terminal walkthrough.

Every public piece requires evidence, a reviewer, date and an appropriately accurate development or regulatory disclosure.

## Release and measurement checklist

- [ ] Build public-only and run the existing quality and product tests on exact PR head.
- [ ] Verify desktop/mobile layout, accessibility, canonical URLs and social metadata.
- [ ] Confirm no production contact or checkout path is silently downgraded.
- [ ] Verify actual public deployed HTML; a merge is not itself deployment evidence.
- [ ] Collect baseline organic impressions, clicks and verified conversions before promising growth.

**Not part of this marketing release:** payment activation, third-party account creation, marketing pixels, paid adverts or unconsented marketing messages.
