Written commercial branding approval must be obtained and retained before adding any UPS mark or proprietary photo to a NovaPharm marketing page.

Owner's assertion is not the same as contract and trademark proof. No logo lockup, `official partner`, or owned-fleet claim is published here.

Do not claim NovaPharm runs the provider's warehouses, owns provider aircraft or vehicles, guarantees worldwide service, or has blanket WDA(H), GDP, import, PLPI or country licences.
